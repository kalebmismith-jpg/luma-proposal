What I noticed

Six things I'd love to fix

It's hard to find you on Instagram

Your website doesn't link to your Instagram, and if you search "lumasociety" you land on someone else's personal account.

What I'd do

Add your Instagram to the website, and send your bio link straight to the application.

The photos say "dinner club"

Your website says Luma is so much more than dinner, but a lot of the photos are food and wine. The membership gallery has no people in it, and the testimonials don't have names.

What I'd do

Show the friendships! Candid moments at the table, the track day, trips, and members (the ones who want to be!) sharing why they joined.

Long quiet stretches

Lately there's been about three weeks between posts. Instagram shows your posts to more people when you post on a steady schedule.

What I'd do

A weekly posting schedule, planned a month ahead.

Some questions go unanswered

A question on your latest reel has been waiting three days for a reply. People notice when questions about who can join don't get answered.

What I'd do

Reply to comments and messages within one business day, so nobody is left waiting.

Your best stories aren't out there yet

Your members serve on boards at St. Jude, AdventHealth Foundation, 13 Ugly Men, BayCare and St. Joseph's, and six companies sponsored the 2026 Gala. I didn't see either of those on your feed.

What I'd do

Regular posts about the good work members do, plus a thank-you shoutout for each sponsor.

Highlights are sorted by month

Highlights like "SEPT '26" and "OCT '26" get old fast and don't tell someone new what Luma is.

What I'd do

Highlights that last: About, Membership, Philanthropy, Gala and How to Apply.

Bigger ideas

When we post with a chef, venue or sponsor, their followers see Luma too. MAISON alone has 21.5K followers!

Make sponsors want to come back

When sponsors see real views on their shoutouts, signing them up for the next gala gets a lot easier.

Start a LinkedIn page

I couldn't find one. It's where founders and investors spend their workday, and members can share posts with their own networks.

Track what really matters

A simple monthly report: how many people saw your posts, visited your page, and clicked through to apply.
